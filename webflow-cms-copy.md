## FAQ 1
**Title:** How does the TikTok name generator work?
**Body:** Enter a keyword or your name, pick a content category, and our TikTok name generator creates ten unique handle ideas in seconds. It blends your input with patterns that suit your niche, then formats every result to TikTok's username rules so the ideas are ready to use.

## FAQ 2
**Title:** What are TikTok's username rules?
**Body:** TikTok usernames can be 2 to 24 characters and may use only lowercase letters, numbers, periods, and underscores. Spaces, emojis, and other symbols are not allowed, a username cannot start or end with a period, and it cannot contain two periods in a row. Every handle we generate already follows these rules.

## FAQ 3
**Title:** Are the generated TikTok usernames available?
**Body:** The generator creates ideas, not availability checks, because TikTok does not offer a public username lookup. That is why we add a check link to each handle that opens its TikTok profile URL, so you can confirm whether it is open before you claim it. Popular combinations may already be taken.

## FAQ 4
**Title:** What makes a good TikTok username?
**Body:** A good TikTok username is short, easy to spell, and reflects your content or personality. Handles that a viewer can remember after seeing them once tend to get searched and tagged more often. Use the category picker to keep suggestions relevant to your niche, and avoid long strings of numbers that are hard to share.

## FAQ 5
**Title:** Can I use this for a business or brand account?
**Body:** Yes. Pick the Business category, or enter your brand name as the keyword, to generate handles suited to a company account. If you want the same name across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, check availability on each platform, since their username rules differ slightly.

## FAQ 6

**Body:** As many as you like. Each run returns ten fresh handle ideas, and you can regenerate as often as you want. There is no login, and the tool is free to use.
